## Description

```
QR CODE FOUNDRY helps you design branded QR code pages that look professional — not plain black-and-white squares.

Create QR codes for everyday needs:
• Website URLs
• Wi‑Fi networks
• Plain text
• Email, phone & SMS
• Contact cards (vCard)
• Google Review pages

Customize every page to match your brand:
• Add your logo at the top
• Edit titles, subtitles and button text
• Choose colors for page, QR and frame
• Style modules as square, round or dots
• Add a thin, rounded, bold or double frame

Preview live as you edit, then export in high quality to share or save to Photos.

Built for creators, shops, restaurants, events and small businesses who want QR codes that feel polished and on-brand.

Forge your codes. Forge your brand.
```
